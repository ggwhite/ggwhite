Hi, I'm **White** (ggwhite), a senior backend engineer with about 15 years of experience.

I mostly write **Go**, **Java** and **Lua**, and I've spent much of my career building and running **high-concurrency game platforms**: real-time services, cluster RPC, caching and data stores that have to stay fast under heavy load.

Lately I've also been building an **AI-assisted development workflow**: coding agents, review and verification loops, and the tooling that keeps them honest.

## About this blog

These are my TIL (Today I Learned) notes. Each post covers one problem I actually hit: what went wrong, the root cause, and the fix. Most posts are written in Traditional Chinese, with code and terms in English.
